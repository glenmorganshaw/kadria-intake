    First time? Six short steps
    
    
      
      
    1. Install Obsidian (free) from the App Store, Google Play or obsidian.md, and open it.
      2. 
      
    2. Create a vault called Kadria. If it offers Sync, skip it.
      3. 
      
    3. Exit Restricted mode: Settings (gear), Community plugins, tap Exit Restricted mode.
      4. 
      
    4. Add My Little Guy: Browse, install and enable BRAT. Then Settings, BRAT, Add beta plugin, paste glenhiggy-sketch/My-Little-Guy, leave Version alone, Add plugin, and check My Little Guy is ON.
        
      Shortcuts (only work on the device with Obsidian): open BRAT · add My Little Guy
      5. 
      
    5. Come back to this page and tap Open in Obsidian. Your character note is created.
      6. 
      
    6. Spell cards: your note shows spells as cards read from spell files. Use the Kadria Spell Cards zip from your emailed sheet (move its Books folder into your vault).
      7.
