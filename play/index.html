First time? Three steps

    
    
      
  1. Install Obsidian (free) from obsidian.md or your app store. Open it and create a vault.
    2. 
      
  2. In Obsidian go to Settings → Community plugins, turn them on, then Browse and install BRAT and enable it.
    3. 
      
  3. In BRAT choose Add a beta plugin and paste glenhiggy-sketch/My-Little-Guy, then Add. My Little Guy appears in your plugin list: enable it.
    4. 
    

    
Then come back to this page (scan the QR on your sheet again) and tap Open in Obsidian. Your character note is created and linked to your DM's sheet.
